Service level (SLA)

Availability and dependencies

Access depends on GhostCLI infrastructure and model providers. A reachable gateway does not guarantee that every model is available. Maintenance and third-party failures may affect the service.

Applicable conditions

Refer to the terms of use and any specific agreement for your subscription for applicable conditions. This page does not establish a new uptime percentage guarantee, resolution deadline or service credit policy.

How to report an issue

Send support the time and time zone, model used, error code and request identifier, if available. Do not include your password, API key or unnecessary personal content.

Privacy and LGPD

Data used by the service

The platform uses account, billing and usage information to provide access, process payments and protect the service. Requests sent to models are processed by the providers involved in generating the response.

Cookies and measurement technologies

The website uses cookies for sessions, language preferences and campaign attribution. It also integrates Meta and UTMify measurement technologies. Contact the channel below for questions about how this data is processed.

Your rights

Under Brazil’s LGPD, you may request confirmation and access, corrections, information about data sharing and, where applicable, portability, anonymization, blocking or deletion. You may also withdraw consent when it is the basis for processing.

Requests and retention

Send your request from the email linked to your account and describe the right you wish to exercise. Identity verification may be necessary. Deletion is subject to legal retention exceptions; ask support for details about the data and retention periods applicable to your case.
